Travel apps

Unlock this phone

Enter your travel passphrase once. It stays unlocked on this phone, offline too, until you lock it.

Your apps

Put each one on your home screen
  1. Open the app from here.
  2. In Chrome, tap ⋮ then Add to Home screen (or Install).
  3. It gets its own icon and opens in its own window.

Everyday shortcuts

For home: these open your live apps on claude.ai, each with its own icon and window. Open one, then tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

Your saved work

    How this works
    1. Before you fly: ask Claude to "pack my apps for travel". You get a travel pack file. Tap Load travel pack here and choose it.
    2. While away: use the apps as normal. Everything saves on this phone.
    3. Back home: tap Make return pack and send the file to Claude with "unpack my travel changes". Only what you changed is merged in, and nothing newer in your account is overwritten.

    Make a return pack every few days while away, and keep it in your Files app or Google Drive. It's also your backup if anything happens to this phone.

    Ask Claude later

    Claude features are switched off while you're away. Jot down anything you want Claude to do, and send the whole list when you're back.
